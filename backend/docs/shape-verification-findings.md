# Shape verification findings (Task 3)

Generated by running `dotnet run -- verify-shapes "C:/Users/kry_2/Desktop/mappa trackmania/riferimenti"`
against the 9 real Trackmania reference maps, plus a set of throwaway offset-sweep
experiments described below. This is a human-judgment review, not an automated
pass/fail — every conclusion cites the actual match/total counts the tool printed.

**Important caveat that shapes every finding below**: `ShapeHypothesisVerifier.IsPlainShapeInstance`
matches by `name.EndsWith(shapeSuffix)`. This means the "Straight" bucket, for example,
also sweeps in `PlatformTechSlope2Straight` and every `*WallStraight` variant, because
those names also end in "Straight" — they are not excluded by the `Tilt/Chicane/Diag/Loop`
marker list. This is existing, tested (Task 2) behavior that I did not modify. Where this
inflates or dilutes the raw aggregate numbers, I broke occurrences down by exact block name
(via a throwaway `DiagCommand.cs`, deleted before commit — see "Method" below) to see what's
really being measured, and I say so explicitly per shape.

## Method

1. Ran `verify-shapes` with the brief's 8 starting-guess candidates (the exact code committed
   in `VerifyShapesCommand.cs`). Full output is pasted in `task-3-report.md`.
2. For every shape whose aggregate match rate was weak, ran a throwaway sweep: temporarily
   added extra `ShapeHypothesis` entries to `VerifyShapesCommand.cs` (same `ShapeSuffix`,
   different `dx/dy/dz`) covering the 4 cardinal single-axis offsets (and diagonals for
   `Curve1`), rebuilt, ran, recorded the match/total, then reverted the file to the exact
   brief-given content before committing.
3. For shapes where the aggregate was suspicious, added a second throwaway command
   (`DiagCommand.cs`, temporarily wired into `Program.cs` as a `diag` subcommand) that
   reuses the existing public `ShapeHypothesisVerifier.IsPlainShapeInstance` and
   `GridRotation.Rotate` to print match/total **per exact block name** instead of
   aggregated per shape-suffix. This is what surfaced the `EndsWith` contamination above.
   Both files were deleted and `Program.cs` reverted before the final commit — the
   committed code is exactly what the brief specifies for Step 1/Step 2.

All raw numbers referenced below are taken verbatim from console output that is also
pasted in full in `task-3-report.md`.

## Straight

**Verdict: UNCONFIRMED / deferred. The X-axis is ruled out; the sign of the Z-axis forward direction is not.**

*(Revised after review — see "Revision note" at the end of this document for why the original

The 516-occurrence aggregate is dominated by `PlatformTechSlope2Straight` (259 of 516,
half the bucket) — a slope ramp shape, not a flat straight, swept in only because its name
also ends in "Straight". Excluding that (it's evaluated on its own merits under
"Slope2Straight" below), the true straight/wall-straight family names all score decisively
higher at `dz=1` than at any alternative axis I tried:

| axis | RoadTechStraight | RoadBumpStraight | PlatformIceWallStraight | PlatformTechWallStraight | PlatformPlasticWallStraight |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| dz=+1 | 12/16 (75%) | 8/10 (80%) | 52/67 (78%) | 70/108 (65%) | 24/43 (56%) |
| dz=-1 | 8/16 (50%) | 7/10 (70%) | 53/67 (79%) | 71/108 (66%) | 24/43 (56%) |
| dx=+1 | 3/16 (19%) | 0/10 (0%) | 3/67 (4%) | 0/108 (0%) | 1/43 (2%) |
| dx=-1 | 0/16 (0%) | 0/10 (0%) | 3/67 (4%) | 0/108 (0%) | 0/43 (0%) |

The X-axis is decisively ruled out for every name (0–19% at `dx=±1` vs. 50–80% at `dz=±1`) —
that part of the original reasoning holds. But the sign of the Z-axis is not resolved by this
data. Weighting by occurrence count: `RoadTechStraight` + `RoadBumpStraight` (the two genuine
road pieces, N=16+10=26, about 11% of the 254-block sample excluding the contaminating slope
name) favor `dz=+1` (75%/80% vs 50%/70%). But the three Wall families
(`PlatformIceWallStraight`, `PlatformTechWallStraight`, `PlatformPlasticWallStraight`,
N=67+108+43=218, about 86% of the sample) either tie almost exactly between the two signs
(79% vs 78%, 66% vs 65%) or lean marginally toward `dz=-1`, and `PlatformGrassWallStraight`
(N=10) favors `dz=-1` outright (30% vs 10%). Combined, roughly 89% of the sample either ties
or leans the opposite way from the 11% that clearly favors `dz=+1`.

The original version of this finding treated the Wall majority as "decorative fill" and
discounted it in favor of the small Road minority, on the unevidenced assertion that Wall
variants are decorative more often. That is the same symmetric-tie pattern I correctly read
as "not real directional evidence, likely non-track reuse" for `Slope2Straight` and `Start`
below — applying a different standard here just because the tie happens to sit close to a
plausible-looking value would be inconsistent. I'm withdrawing the confirmation: the data
rules out the X-axis cleanly, but does not establish which Z sign is the true forward
direction. This needs either more reference maps with a larger genuine-road sample, or an
independent way to confirm the Wall families' true connector semantics before they can be
used as corroborating (rather than contradicting) evidence.

## Slope2Straight

**Verdict: UNCONFIRMED / deferred — explicit reasoning below, not just a low percentage.**

Starting-guess aggregate: 3/262 (1%) at `(dx=0, dy=1, dz=1)`. Swept `dz=±1` against
`dy ∈ {0, 1, -1, 2, -2}`, and separately `dx=±1` against the same `dy` range, for the
dominant name `PlatformTechSlope2Straight` (259 of 262 occurrences):

| axis | dy=0 | dy=+1 | dy=-1 | dy=+2 | dy=-2 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| dz=+1 | 0/259 (0%) | 3/259 (1%) | 16/259 (6%) | 89/259 (34%) | 37/259 (14%) |
| dz=-1 | 3/259 (1%) | 2/259 (1%) | 20/259 (8%) | 64/259 (25%) | 76/259 (29%) |
| dx=+1 | **204/259 (79%)** | 1/259 | 2/259 | 2/259 | 0/259 |
| dx=-1 | **204/259 (79%)** | 1/259 | 2/259 | 2/259 | 1/259 |

The best forward/climb-axis result (`dz=+1, dy=+2`) only reaches 34% — not confirmable. The
strong result is on the X axis at `dy=0`: 79%, and — critically — **identical** for both
`dx=+1` and `dx=-1` (204/259 both ways). A real forward connector shouldn't match equally in
both opposite directions; a block has one "next" piece, not two. This is the same symmetric
signature I flagged under "Start": it reads as a repeated row of identical slope blocks at
constant height (a decorative wall/terrain feature, most likely in the single very large
scenery map `Alpha Valley 1`, 15,803 blocks total), not a climbing track ramp. Since the v1
"Slope2Straight" shape is supposed to represent a track connector that climbs one Y level,
and the dominant evidence in these 9 maps looks like non-track decorative reuse of the same
mesh, I'm treating this as unconfirmed rather than reporting a misleading offset. This is
exactly the "mismatches look systematic, not like genuine dead-ends" case the brief calls
out. `PlatformIceSlope2Straight` (N=3) shows 100% at two different, mutually exclusive
offsets (`dy=-1,dz=+1` and `dy=+2,dz=-1`) depending on which was tried — with N=3 that's
noise, not a signal.
